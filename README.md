## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Master's student in **Artificial Intelligence** at Soochow University, working with the OpenNLG Research Group. My research focuses on **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** and **Large Language Models (LLM)**.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Suzhou University of Science and Technology, ranking **1st out of 82** in my major, and was awarded the **National Scholarship** in 2025.

### 🔬 Research Interests

- 🤖 Large Language Models (LLM)
- 📝 Natural Language Generation (NLG)
